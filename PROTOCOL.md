## Calendar and cohort

Target quarters 2000Q1-2024Q4. A forecast origin is the published RTDSM quarterly vintage for that same quarter. Source documentation places these vintages in the middle month of the quarter (February, May, August, November); it does not establish an exact trading day or intraday access. Call this a snapshot-information-set experiment, not an exact-day historical trading simulation.

For each target q, historical inputs must contain exactly 21 positive GDP levels for q-21 through q-1 in origin vintage q, yielding 20 quarterly growth rates. The origin column's last nonmissing observation must be q-1. Missing/stale/future last observations or missing/nonpositive history make the origin ineligible; retain every reason and never fill missing values with zero. Do not interpolate a missing vintage or silently shorten the history. Keep the full planned 100-quarter denominator and exclusion ledger.

Use fixed later vintage 2026Q3 for revised-input and revised-target counterfactuals. Verify that this exact column exists; do not choose another after seeing results. Compute every growth rate from adjacent levels within one vintage, never splice levels across rebased dollar units. Later-vintage inputs are deliberately unavailable at earlier origins and are hindsight controls, not valid deployable forecasts. Use exactly the same origin dates, history length, method family and evaluation subsets across regimes.

Source documentation identifies exceptional GDP publication timing around government shutdowns. Exclude 2018Q4 as a target and 2019Q1 as an origin before collection; their first-release timing/information set is not comparable under the standard quarterly convention. The documented 2013Q4 timing issue lies in the unscored separation block. Preserve these exclusions, including whether source availability would independently fail. Do not treat them as successful forecasts. Other missingness is a source-qualified exclusion, not a repaired zero. Do not extend the cohort or modify calendar rules after outcomes.

## Target construction and source cross-check

The early target is the annualized growth rate computed within vintage q+1 from GDP(q)/GDP(q-1). Documentation identifies its last observation as the advance estimate under the standard quarterly convention. Compare this value with the separate First series for each non-exceptional planned target. The second target uses the identical formula and observation pair within 2026Q3. Do not call the latter final truth: it is one fixed later revision with possible definition and measurement changes.

Growth is 100*((level_t/level_t_minus 1)^4-1), in annualized percentage points. Parse numeric workbook cells using the runtime's decimal rendering of stored Excel numbers; preserve those tokens and cell coordinates. Source-level float-reader cross-check tolerance is max(1e-12,2e-16*absolute level), a representation allowance rather than source accuracy. Early-target agreement with the independently supplied First series must be within 1e-8 percentage point before quantization, a conservative float/power representation allowance; retain every difference and stop on a larger unexplained mismatch. Missing First values remain unavailable. Do not relax either tolerance after a failure.

Convert the exact rational growth formula to a 12 decimal-place fixed representation using round-half-even. This bounds arithmetic size; it does not claim twelve-digit economic accuracy. All subsequent forecasts, absolute/squared losses and comparisons use exact rational arithmetic on these fixed inputs. Display metrics to 12 decimal places; compute RMSE with 50-digit Decimal square root before half-even display. Headline prose may use 3 decimals with explicit units.

## Six candidates and temporal selection

Candidate 0 is zero growth. Candidates 1-5 blend the mean of the 20 available growth rates and the latest available growth: forecast=(1-w)*mean 20+w*last, where w is 0,1/4,1/2,3/4,1. These are fixed simple benchmarks; no fitted AR coefficients, feature search, imputation, embeddings or fine-tuning. Weight-grid endpoints and simple baselines remain visible. No random seed is required because arithmetic and selection are deterministic.

Retain all four regimes: real-time inputs/early target; real-time inputs/later target; revised inputs/early target; revised inputs/later target. Primary selection metric is equal-quarter MAE in annualized percentage points; report MSE(pp squared) and RMSE(pp). Exact MAE ties choose the lower candidate index. Select separately within each regime using development quarters 2000Q1-2011Q4(48 planned), then commit protocol, source snapshot, transformation code, development exports and selection dependency hashes before computing held metrics. All 2012-2013 quarters are unscored separation observations; their histories may appear as already-available lagged data for later origins. Held quarters 2014Q1-2024Q4 have 44 planned and 42 after the two fixed timing exclusions, subject to source eligibility. No held outcome may change candidates, parameters, metric, subgroups or narrative claim.

The deployable information-boundary benchmark is real-time inputs. Separate the effect of changing the target while holding its prediction fixed from the effect of changing inputs while holding the target fixed. Export every regime/candidate loss and selection, including ties and negative comparisons. Report whether selected or full-candidate rankings change, not only whether the best score improves.
